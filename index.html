Our Story

1961

A Tucson Original

Henry "Pat" Patterson opened Pat's Chili Dogs in the heart of Tucson's Barrio Hollywood neighborhood with a simple vision: great chili dogs, honest burgers, and nothing fancy. The original menu featured hot dogs, hamburgers, chicken, fish, shrimp, fries, and Pat's signature chili — and that's pretty much what you'll still find today.

1963

Charlie Joins the Team

A 15-year-old Charlie Hernandez walked through the doors and started working alongside Pat. He learned every detail of the operation — the recipes, the customers, the rhythm of the kitchen. He never left.

1979

Passing the Torch

When Pat Patterson was ready to move on, he sold the restaurant to Charlie, the person who knew it best. Pat's parting advice was simple: take care of the customers and keep the food consistent. Charlie has lived by those words for over four decades.

Today

A Family Legacy

Charlie still works the counter daily. His daughter and grandchildren have joined the team, preparing to carry Pat's into the future. The staff still memorize your order — no writing anything down. As Charlie says, "I don't want nobody writing down any orders." That's how Pat's has always done it.
